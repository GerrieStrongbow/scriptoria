Privacy Policy for Scriptoria

Last updated: August 14, 2025

Overview

Scriptoria ("we", "our", or "the app") is a document scanning application that respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

Scriptoria operates entirely offline on your device. All documents, images, and PDFs you create are stored locally on your device only.

Data Storage

  • All scanned documents are saved locally on your device
  • No data is uploaded to any external servers
  • No cloud storage or backup services are used by the app
  • You have complete control over your documents

Permissions

Scriptoria requires the following permissions to function:

  • Camera: Used solely for scanning documents. No images are transmitted outside your device.
  • Storage: Used to save scanned documents and PDFs on your device.

Third-Party Services

Scriptoria does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data. When you use the share functionality, you are directly sharing files from your device storage using your device's native sharing capabilities.

Children's Privacy

Scriptoria does not collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Security

Since all data remains on your device, the security of your documents depends on your device's security settings. We recommend using device-level security features like screen locks and encryption.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please create an issue on our GitHub repository or contact us through the Google Play Store.

Compliance

This privacy policy is designed to comply with Google Play Store requirements and applicable data protection regulations.
