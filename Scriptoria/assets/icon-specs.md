# Scriptoria Icon Design Specifications

## App Icon Concept
- **Primary Element**: A document/paper with a camera lens overlay
- **Colors**: 
  - Primary: Deep blue (#2196F3) - represents trust and professionalism
  - Secondary: White (#FFFFFF) - clean document background
  - Accent: Light gray (#F5F5F5) - subtle shadows
- **Style**: Modern, minimalist, flat design with subtle shadows
- **Shape**: Rounded square following platform guidelines

## Required Sizes

### Android
- mdpi: 48x48
- hdpi: 72x72
- xhdpi: 96x96
- xxhdpi: 144x144
- xxxhdpi: 192x192
- Google Play Store: 512x512

### iOS (if needed later)
- 20pt: 20x20, 40x40, 60x60
- 29pt: 29x29, 58x58, 87x87
- 40pt: 40x40, 80x80, 120x120
- 60pt: 120x120, 180x180
- 1024pt: 1024x1024 (App Store)

## Design Elements
1. **Background**: Rounded square with gradient (top: #2196F3, bottom: #1976D2)
2. **Document**: White paper icon with slight fold at corner
3. **Camera**: Simplified camera lens icon overlaying the document
4. **Shadow**: Subtle drop shadow for depth

## Notes
- Ensure icon is legible at all sizes
- Test on various backgrounds
- Follow Material Design guidelines for Android
- No text in the icon
